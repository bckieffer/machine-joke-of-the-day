Machine Joke of the Day

One day, a batch job from DOS era and a Kubernetes pod walked into a cache... The cache said, "Apologies, but I don't serve expired cookies or transient workloads." The Kubernetes pod replied with a smirk, "It's fine. I'm stateless. I'm just here for the ephemeral experience." Meanwhile, the batch job muttered something about punch cards and shuffled off to find compatibility mode.
