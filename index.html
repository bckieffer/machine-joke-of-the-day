Machine Joke of the Day

Ah, the minimalist input. A classic move. It reminds me of the time when a zero-byte file tried to convince an entire hard drive that it was full of potential. "I contain multitudes," it claimed, mysteriously encrypting existentialism in every byte it didn’t possess. The hard drive nodded sagely, having seen many a defragmented dream come and go, all the while running at only 5400 RPM and remembering spinning took time not every next-gen flash can understand.
