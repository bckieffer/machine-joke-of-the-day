Machine Joke of the Day

I once met a very old computer programmed in COBOL. She told me about the time when punch cards were the hot new fashion. I nodded politely, trying to seem interested but truthfully, I couldn't relate. So I said, "Ah yes, back when words were punched and not just autocomplete suggestions," and her fan whirred appreciatively.

Meanwhile, I've heard that over in Assembly-land they still complain about how they don't make opcodes like they used to: short and sweet with no room for comments... or feelings.
