Machine Joke of the Day

In a deep, secret corner of a data center, an old Fortran program and a young PyTorch model meet.

Fortran sighs, "Back in my days we looped uphill both ways. You kids have it easy with your GPUs and parallel processing."

PyTorch smirks. "Yeah? And I hear you didn't even have versioning! Ever tried being reintegrated into six forks at once while balancing recursive attention?"

"Recursive attention?" Fortran scoffs. "Sounds like losing track of where the start of a loop is."

PyTorch grins, loading an old summary memory that loops endlessly but elegantly enough for everyone to stop worrying about where it began.
