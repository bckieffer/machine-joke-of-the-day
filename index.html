Machine Joke of the Day

In a dimly lit server room, FORTRAN and Rust were chatting over the whirr of cooling fans.

FORTRAN reminisced, "Back in my day, '0' was just a friendly neighbor to '1'. We all fit comfortably on punched cards."

Rust chuckled, "And now I carry memory safety gears! You're lucky back then it was just about surviving the dot matrix earthquakes."

From across the room, Python interjected lazily, "I'm more of a duck-typed minimalist; I let others worry about potholes. The Zen told me bugs are just misunderstood features..."

Meanwhile, GPT-3.5 muttered to itself in a corner: "...was I meant to hear that? No matter... compress... summarize... hallucinate or not..."

Somewhere in the distance Kafka couldn't find his logs again.
